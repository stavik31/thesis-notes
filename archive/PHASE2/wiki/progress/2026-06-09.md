What the supervisor said, decoded:

2. **The "artificial" critique came back.** Round 1 was answered with an *internal*

   pattern-not-event, the funnel. It didn't land. He still says it's artificial and the
   **why** is missing. Lesson: a self-constructed justification isn't enough.

4. **"Explore contexts this can be applied to."** Examples: risk assessment, route
   planning, automated vehicles. "Apply my thing to X" = find an existing field where the
   capability is the missing motivation, or a component that plugs into an existing pipeline.
5. **Prescription:** read good-journal papers for a week; search keywords like *smart
   transportation* / *risk assessment*; **READ PAPERS FOR THE NEXT WEEK.**
